# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Auditors, legal counsel, analysts, operational teams, and business professionals reviewing complex multi-page documents (contracts, financial statements, tax invoices, insurance schedules, and corporate research reports).

## Product Purpose

DocFin is an intelligent document analysis and extraction platform. It turns dense, complex, multi-page static documents into clear answers, structured CSV data tables, executive summaries, and risk assessments in seconds.

## Positioning

Unlike generic chatbots or rigid optical character recognition tools, DocFin combines spatial layout coordinate grounding with Google Gemini 1.5/2.0 Flash Multimodal AI to extract fine print, calculate tax credits, flag contract liabilities, and answer questions with verified page coordinate citations—guaranteeing zero hallucinations.

## Operating Context

Modern corporate, legal, accounting, and compliance environments where documents arrive as PDFs or high-resolution scans and need fast, grounded verification and tabular extraction without manual re-keying.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Universal multi-domain ingestion (Contracts, Reports, Statements, Invoices, Insurance Policies) up to 50MB.
- Automated layout coordinate OCR and structural JSON extraction via Gemini Multimodal AI.
- Conversational RAG engine with strict page coordinate citations.
- 4 Super-action intelligence routines (Clauses & Risks, Executive Summaries, CSV Data Extraction, Fee & Discrepancy Audits).
- Collapsible document history tracking active and past audited files (`xxx.pdf`).
- Single-column focused authentication with Google OAuth and Email/Password flows.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: DocFin (Document Intelligence)
- Voice: Intelligent, minimal, trustworthy, professional, and clear.
- Core Accent: DocFin Blue (`#2563EB`) with high-contrast emerald action pills (`#1E7145`).

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified Next.js 16 (Turbopack) web application with zero build errors.
- Fully working Gemini Multimodal `/api/analyze` and Conversational RAG `/api/chat` endpoints.
- High-contrast light and dark theme design tokens configured in `globals.css`.

## Product Principles

1. **Coordinate Grounding First**: Never hallucinate numbers or terms; anchor findings to page coordinates.
2. **Speed & Clarity**: Deliver plain-English executive summaries and tabular data immediately.
3. **Approachable Modern SaaS**: Keep the canvas serene, high-contrast, and focused on the document without decorative noise.
